A NUMBERED LOAD ORDER SCHEME FOR BALDUR'S GATE 3, FROM THE DATA
VOLO, October 2026. Derived from 160 submitted working load orders and the Nexus and mod.io catalogues.
Hundreds are a load order, in the order working orders put them. Tens are filing unless marked measured.
basis: hundred = a top-level family; measured = the corpus orders it; theme = placed by judgement, no header evidence; catch-all = the rest of its hundred.
Positions quoted are fractions of the order, 0 at the top and 1 at the bottom. "Before X 70 percent" means players had the pair that way round 70 percent of the time.
The page this comes from: https://volobg3.com/CaranudTemp/
